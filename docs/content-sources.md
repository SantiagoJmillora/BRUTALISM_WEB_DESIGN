## Albarrada de Mompox — key figures

- 2.7 km intervention, ~180,000 m², in a UNESCO World Heritage town ([ArchDaily](https://www.archdaily.com/es/902377/revitalizacion-de-la-albarrada-de-mompox-ganador-de-la-iii-bienal-latinoamericana-de-arquitectura-de-paisaje-blap)).
- `[VERIFY]` Construction/completion dates are inconsistent across sources (Landezine says built 2014; awards 2016–2018; [mincit.gov.co](https://www.mincit.gov.co/prensa/noticias/turismo/gobierno-entrega-obra-eje-urbano-albarrada-mompox) reports a related urban-axis handover in Dec 2024). Verify before publishing any date.
